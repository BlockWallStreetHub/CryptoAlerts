How the score works
  • Timeframes: pick Daily, Weekly or Monthly. All averages are EMAs (exponential moving averages, seeded with a simple average the way TradingView does). Daily 20/50/100/200-day EMAs, 120-day Fibonacci swing, 30-day divergence window. Weekly 20/50/100/200-week EMAs, 52-week swing, 12-week divergence window. Monthly 6/12/18/24-month EMAs (200 months is more history than exists), 24-month swing, 6-month divergence window. The "D · W · M" column always shows all three at once. Weekly and monthly need years of history, so they use the free Binance data and only work for Binance-listed tokens.
  • Slow EMA (±1): price above the slowest EMA (200-day, 200-week or 24-month) is an uptrend on that timeframe, below is a downtrend.
  • 50/200 EMA cross (±1): 50 above 200 = golden-cross state, below = death-cross state. Recent crosses are flagged.
  • Chart controls: click a token to open its chart. Zoom picks how many bars to show (the price scale fits only the bars on screen, so an old peak no longer squashes today). Log switches to a log price scale (Auto turns it on when the range is very wide). ⤢ Expand (or double-click the chart) opens it full screen with Daily / Weekly / Monthly tabs. Move the mouse over the chart for exact prices, EMAs and RSI on any bar.
  • EMA stack (±1): bullish when price > 20 > 50 > 100 > 200 (every average in order), bearish when it is fully reversed, otherwise 0. This replaces the old 20/50-only check, so it is stricter and fires less often.
  • 20 EMA readout (not scored on its own): the orange 20 EMA column and chart line show exactly where the fast EMA is, how far price is from it, whether it is rising or falling, and how many closes in a row price has held above or below it. Use the "Near 20 EMA" filter to find tokens testing it right now.
  • RSI (±1): under 30 oversold (+1), over 70 overbought (−1). Neutral in between.
  • Fibonacci (±1): the scanner finds the biggest swing in the lookback window (120 days, 52 weeks or 24 months). Price pulling back into the 0.382–0.786 zone of an up-swing = dip-buy zone (+1). Price bouncing into the same zone of a down-swing = resistance (−1). 0.618 is the "golden pocket".
  • Divergence (±2 regular, ±1 hidden): compares the last two price swing lows/highs to RSI at those points. Regular bullish = price lower low, RSI higher low. Regular bearish = price higher high, RSI lower high. Hidden divergences signal trend continuation. Only counted if the second pivot is recent (30 days, 12 weeks or 6 months).
  • Total: +4 or more Strong Bullish · +2/+3 Bullish · −1 to +1 Neutral · −2/−3 Bearish · −4 or less Strong Bearish.
  • Data: when Binance is reachable, all three timeframes use Binance candles (real highs and lows, free, no key). Signals use the last closed candle unless you tick "include unfinished candle". If Binance is blocked in your browser, or a token is not listed there, the scanner falls back to CoinGecko daily closes (daily timeframe only) or GeckoTerminal (DEX tokens, daily only). Always confirm on a TradingView chart before acting.

Runs the exact scanner scoring (MAs + Fibonacci + RSI divergences) day by day on each coin, and compares it with a simple trend baseline and buy & hold. Binance daily candles are free and need no key; a 5-year run takes about 1–3 minutes. Coins are ranked by market cap using one CoinGecko call.
Read before trusting any number here:
  • The first 200 days of each coin's history are used to warm up the 200-day EMA, so the tested period starts about 200 days after the history begins. With 5 years of Binance data, testing begins around spring 2022 and includes the 2022 bear market. The CoinGecko option only tests about 165 days, which is a single market regime.
  • Survivorship bias. It uses today's top coins by market cap (only those listed on Binance, minus stablecoins and wrapped/staked copies). Coins that collapsed or were delisted aren't in the list, which flatters every column, buy & hold most of all.
  • Trades execute at the next daily close after a signal, with the cost per side applied on every buy and sell. Real small-cap slippage can be much worse than 0.3%.
  • Weekly and monthly runs use all available Binance history (the History box applies to daily only). The slow average needs 200 weeks or 24 months before testing can start, so far fewer coins qualify and monthly has very few trades. Treat those results as rough, not proof.
  • Long-only, equal money per token, idle cash earns nothing. Coins that listed later sit in cash until their data starts. Past results do not predict future results.
